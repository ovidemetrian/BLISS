UX and UI, in full

UX is the journey a person takes through a product: whether they reach their goal, how fast, and how sure they feel on the way. It is mostly invisible. You notice it only when it breaks.

UI is the surface that journey runs across: the buttons, type, colour, spacing and motion. It is what you see and touch, and it is how the product answers back when you touch it.

UX comes first. It decides the structure: what goes where, what happens in what order, and what can be removed. UI then dresses that structure so it is readable, tappable and calm.

You cannot fix a broken flow with nicer pixels. And a perfect flow still fails if the buttons are too small, the text too faint, or the important thing hidden.

After launch, the two stop being a line and become a loop. People touch the UI, you watch where they stumble, and what you learn becomes the next round of UX.

This screen is that idea in practice: a short answer up top that never scrolls, and the whole story only when you ask for it.

HOW BLISS WORKS

One screen, three thirds. Nothing scrolls until you ask.

TOP · THE ANSWER

The answer lands here, short. White outline means ready. A slow rainbow means there’s an answer. Tap Short to cycle Short · Medium · Deep.

MIDDLE · YOUR QUESTION

What you type or say shows up here. Blue means typing; red means listening. Tap Talk to speak, and again to stop.

BOTTOM · THE KEYS

Typewriter keys. The chips add intent: Explain, Compare, Do it, and Are you sure? to make me double-check. Double-tap space for a full stop. The keyboard icon switches to your phone’s own keyboard.

THE RING

The red, yellow and blue ring between the two pieces is my voice. Tap it and I read the answer aloud; the top piece pulses while I speak. Hold it to choose which AI answers, or drag it and watch it float home.

GO FULL SCREEN

Each piece has a nose on its side and an arc in its corner. Tap either: the answer opens from the right to read in full; your question opens from the left to write as much as you like. The back arrow returns.
