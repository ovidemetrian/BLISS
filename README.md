# BLISS — AI Without the Box

A phone interface concept for talking to AI that isn't a chat box.

**Live:** https://ovidemetrian.github.io/BLISS/

## The idea

Most AI apps look the same: a text box at the bottom and bubbles scrolling up. BLISS starts from one rule: *simplicity is bliss.*

- **One screen, three equal thirds.** The answer on top, your question in the middle, the keys at the bottom. Nothing scrolls until you ask for it.
- **Two interlocking pieces.** The answer and the question are two identical shapes, one turned upside down, locked together like puzzle pieces.
- **One chair.** The ring where the two pieces meet is the AI's voice. Tap it to hear the answer, hold it to choose which AI sits there, drag it and it floats back home. The interface stays yours; the intelligence is swappable.
- **Typewriter keys.** Brass-ringed round keys on a staggered layout, with intent chips: *Explain · Compare · Do it · Are you sure?* A switch hands over to the phone's own keyboard when you want it.
- **Go full screen on demand.** Each piece has a nose and a corner arc; tap either to open the whole answer or write a long question.

## Install

Open the live link on your phone and choose **Add to Home Screen** (or **Install app**). It works offline after the first visit.

## Status

Concept prototype. Answers are sample text; only the first AI in the list is marked as connected, and the API form does not send or store anything.
